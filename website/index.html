FAQ

Questions you might have.

Is HoverWord free?

Yes. No in-app purchases, no subscription, no ads — every feature is fully unlocked.

Is my word data uploaded anywhere?

No. All libraries, progress and favorites stay on your Mac . Nothing is uploaded — it runs entirely local.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Pronunciation uses the macOS system TTS engine, and libraries import and store locally — fully offline.

Which systems are supported?

macOS 14 (Sonoma) and later, on both Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

How do I import a wordbook?

Prepare a UTF-8 TXT file, one entry per line, fields separated by tabs (word, phonetic, part of speech and meaning). It validates on import and reports exact line numbers for errors.

Which language pairs are supported?

Language-agnostic by design. Ship with Chinese learning English out of the box, and support English, French, Spanish, German, Japanese, Korean, Italian, Portuguese, Russian and more — any pair.
